# Contributing

Thank you for your interest. The project prioritises reproducible research, clear threat models, privacy-preserving telemetry and conservative interpretation of evidence.

Before submitting a change:

1. Explain the research question and threat model.
2. Use synthetic data and authorised test infrastructure.
3. Include reproducible steps and expected observations.
4. Separate raw observations from conclusions.
5. Document false-positive and false-negative limitations.
6. Add or update tests and schemas where applicable.

Do not submit real credentials, customer prompts or private provider data. Contributions are provided under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence.
